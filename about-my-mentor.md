His main research interests involves developing stochastic models of Calcium and other ion channels in cardiac myocytes. My graduate mentor is Tijesunimi Adeyemi. He is from Nigeria and he enjoys going to Church, where he is a leading member of the choir, listening to music and going to the gym. He is currently studying to get his master's degree at Morgan State University.
